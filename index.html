WHO WE ARE

About InAmigos Foundation

Founded on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO), InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization based in Chhattisgarh and working across India.

The foundation addresses social challenges through volunteers, community partnerships and programs focused on education, food distribution, women’s empowerment, animal welfare, environmental conservation and career development.

“Together, we can build a more inclusive, compassionate and empowered society.”

Credentials & Recognitions

  • Section 8 registered organization
  • 80G & 12A certified
  • CSR-1 registered
  • NITI Aayog registered
  • IAF ISO 9001:2015 certified

Credentials are as stated in the foundation's organizational description.
